**One additional, higher-severity knowledge/evidence bug was found and fixed** (not itself a harmful-outcome case, but caused 5 real, in-catalogue GPU products to be wrongly declared `OUTSIDE_SUPPORTED_DOMAIN`):

| Case IDs | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `exact_23/24/25_*arc*`, `family_only_03_arca770`, `family_only_08_arca750` | The `"intel arc"` brand alias claimed character span `(0,9)` in every "Intel Arc A7xx..." title, overlapping and blocking the `"arc a770"`/`"arc a750"` family-alias span checked immediately after (evidence extraction never reconsiders an already-taken span). Every Intel Arc listing therefore got a brand_token but no product_family_token, and Decision Formation correctly-but-wrongly concluded `OUTSIDE_SUPPORTED_DOMAIN` for a real, in-catalogue GPU family. | Removed `"intel arc"` from `brand_aliases` (`"intel"` alone is sufficient) |

All three fixes are single-entry/single-list, narrow, cited by exact case IDs, and covered by `tests/pue/test_regression_suite.py` (which fails if any of them regresses) — see `data/pue/knowledge/gpu_terms_v0.1.json`'s `change_note` for the full technical narrative. `TERM_VERSION` was bumped `gpu-terms-0.1.0` → `gpu-terms-0.1.1`.

## 5. Remaining non-harmful disagreements (13 cases)

None of these are in a mandatory zero-tolerance category. Grouped by root cause:

- **Misleading-similarity merchandise** (`misleading_01_mousepad`, `misleading_02_tshirt`, `misleading_03_keychain`, `misleading_04_sticker_decal`): a title like "RTX 4090 Gaming Mouse Pad" resolves to `PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED` / `product_form=complete_product` rather than being recognized as non-GPU merchandise. **This is the most significant residual risk found this sprint** — see §8.
- **Decision-type looseness at the CLASSIFIED/AMBIGUOUS/ABSTAINED boundary** (`bracket_02_riser_cable`, `compat_01_case_fits_rtx4090`, `compat_02_cooler_compatible`, `adapter_03_riser_cable_variant`, `abstain_01_contradiction`, `abstain_03_two_families_conflict`, `not_included_01_case_only`, `not_included_03_manual_only`): non-harmful gold-label/implementation disagreements on which of two safe, non-committal outcomes is reached for weakly-worded compatibility/exclusion phrasing.
- **`mobile_04_amd_rx7900m`** (RETRIEVAL_FAILURE): the synthetic mobile-GPU catalogue concept is not retrieved for "AMD Radeon RX 7900M Mobile GPU" — a minor, narrow retrieval-matching gap on one synthetic catalogue concept, not a real-product regression.

## 10. Downstream comparability recommendation

### **LIMITED_ACTIVATION**

**Benchmark version:** `gpu-release-benchmark-0.1.0` · **Capability:** `pue-0.1.0` · **Policy:** `gpu-policy-0.1.0` · **Knowledge:** `gpu-seed-0.1.0`

**Relevant metrics:** release gate PASS (10/10 mandatory checks); 0 harmful errors in both tracked categories (0/29, 0/6); product-form accuracy 1.000 (44/44); comparability accuracy 1.000 (2/2); exact-identification accuracy 1.000 (11/11); explanation faithfulness 1.000 (118/118); Candidate recall@1 0.973 (36/37, 7 catalogue-gap exclusions); classifier/PUE differential strongly favours the PUE (83 corrections vs. 1 regression, 0 avoidable abstentions).

**Harmful error counts:** 0 in the final release run (2 found and fixed during the sprint — see §4).

**Supported providers and domain:** local, deterministic, title-first GPU identification only (NVIDIA RTX 30/40, AMD RX 7000, Intel Arc, plus their accessories/components/packaging/bundles as seeded in `gpu_seed_v0.1.json`); no live-provider dependency.

**Allowed Decision/product-form outputs for downstream use:** `Decision.product_form` and `Decision.comparability_status` may be used **only** to *prevent* deduplication/grouping across product-form boundaries (i.e., stop a water block, replacement part, packaging-only, or bundle listing from being merged with a complete-product GPU listing, and vice versa). This is exactly the behaviour validated at 1.000 product-form accuracy with zero harmful errors.

**Excluded outcomes (do not activate yet):**
- Any decision, on this evidence, that uses PUE output to *positively group* two listings as comparable at the exact-identity level for pricing/matching purposes — that requires more benchmark evidence than the 11-case exact-identification sample here provides.
- Any comparability decision for a title carrying **misleading-similarity signal** (a real GPU model token attached to clearly non-GPU merchandise: mouse pads, apparel, keychains, stickers) — this is the one residual, unaddressed risk this benchmark surfaced (§5); such titles must continue to route through the existing classifier/manual path until a follow-up benchmark demonstrates it is resolved.
- Catalogue-gap products (real GPUs not yet in `gpu_seed_v0.1.json`, e.g. RX 6000-series, RTX 40-series non-SUPER-Ti variants): PUE output for these is honest ("not identified") but must not be treated as a negative comparability signal — it is a knowledge gap, not evidence the product doesn't exist.

**Residual risks:**
1. Misleading-similarity merchandise (§5, §8) is not yet reliably distinguished from an actual GPU listing.
2. The seed catalogue is small (~36 products); exact-identification coverage (11/118 gold-strict cases) is real but limited.
3. One synthetic mobile-GPU retrieval gap (`mobile_04_amd_rx7900m`) — narrow, not evaluated as blocking.

**Monitoring requirements:** re-run the release benchmark (`arb pue benchmark`) on every `gpu_terms_v0.1.json`/`gpu_seed_v0.1.json`/policy change before any activation-scope change; track the two mandatory harmful-error rates and candidate recall@1 as the primary ongoing health signals.

**Rollback condition:** any observed accessory-to-complete-product or packaging-to-complete-product error in production-adjacent shadow data, or any new benchmark version showing either mandatory-gate rate regress above 0, immediately reverts to `DO_NOT_ACTIVATE` for the affected scope.

**Evidence required for wider activation (e.g. `ACTIVATE_PRODUCT_FORM_PROTECTION` at the positive-comparability level, or beyond):** (a) a follow-up benchmark iteration specifically targeting misleading-similarity/non-GPU-merchandise titles with a demonstrated fix; (b) catalogue coverage expansion evaluated as its own benchmark version (never silently, per non-goals); (c) a second, independently-authored labelled sample to reduce reliance on a single hand-authored 118-case dataset.

No pricing, profit, ROI, demand, or purchasing-recommendation logic is touched by this recommendation or by any PUE module in this sprint — that boundary remains structurally enforced (`test_invariants.py::test_invariant_no_commercial_fields`) and is unaffected by this report.

## 11. Highest-value next capability (evidence-based)

The single largest, most concretely evidenced gap this benchmark surfaces is **misleading-similarity / non-GPU-merchandise rejection** (§5, §10): 4 of the 13 non-harmful disagreements, and the one category with a plausible path to a *future* harmful classification (a merchandise item being treated as comparable to a real GPU) if downstream comparability were activated without addressing it first. This is a narrower, better-evidenced next target than broad catalogue expansion (explicitly out of scope for Sprint 3) or new retrieval infrastructure (no retrieval-latency or -recall bottleneck was observed - recall@1 already 0.973).

## 12. Known limitations / deferred decisions

- No case in `gpu_release_benchmark_v0.1.json` is gold-labelled `avoidable_if_abstained=true`; the avoidable-abstention-rate metric is well-defined (denominator 0 → reported as `null`, never fabricated as 0/0=0) but has no exercised case in this version. A future benchmark version should add at least one such case.
- `harmful_false_match_rate`'s denominator is also 0 in this dataset version (no case combines `forbidden_harmful_outcomes: ["harmful_false_match"]` with a forbidden catalogue product actually being selected) — reported honestly as `null`, not fabricated.
- The benchmark and this report are a first (v0.1) iteration; per brief §4.4, any future correction to a label requires a new `benchmark_version` and a documented adjudication in the dataset's `corrections` array — none were needed for this initial version (all cases in `gpu_release_benchmark_v0.1.json` are original, unrevised labels).
